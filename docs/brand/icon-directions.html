The chandlo, raised

It was cradled in the curve at cy 55, which read as resting rather than sitting. The mark is now taller and a touch wider, and the chandlo has come up to cy 45 — two and a half units above the tilak's optical centre, riding in the open channel between the arms rather than in the cradle.

What changed

Same mark, side by side. The old one is not wrong so much as bottom-heavy — the dot and the cradle merge into a single mass and the arms read as empty.

Above the centre

The hairline in the third specimen is the tilak's optical centre — the midpoint of the painted mark, cap to cradle — and it crosses the chandlo below the dot's middle. Raising the dot puts it in the open channel between the arms and leaves a clear band of cloth under it, so the cradle reads as a stroke that turns rather than as a bowl holding something. The mark gets lighter, which is the whole difference.

Keep climbing, or stop here?

The dot has moved up three times running, so this range opens upward rather than bracketing the current pick — if 45 is better than 47.5, 43 or 41 may be better still. Each shown large and at 40px on both grounds. Past about 41 the dot starts to detach from the cradle and read as a separate object, which is the failure mode at that end.
